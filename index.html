How the model works

GoOperator is the system of record for every load, driver, and truck.

01 — SOURCING FREIGHT

Loads find the truck, not the other way around.

GoOperator pulls available loads from Amazon Relay, DAT, and Uber Freight — plus direct Milwaukee shippers — and surfaces the ones that fit each truck and driver location. No more manual board-scanning eating the day.

02 — RUNNING THE TRUCK

The driver drives. The software carries the paperwork.

Every truck runs the GoOperator driver app: load assignment, navigation handoff, document capture, proof of delivery, and status updates — all in one guided flow. The driver's only job is to drive and confirm.

03 — RUNNING THE COMPANY

Dispatch, compliance, and settlement in one place.

On the operations side, GoOperator handles dispatch, compliance tracking — licenses, medical cards, drug-test schedules, qualification files — and settlement: what each driver earns against their truck's cost, and what GoOperations nets as the platform.

The truck-as-asset loop

Every rented truck is built to fund itself — and prove when it's worth owning.

STEP 01
